Welcome to Salt & Sage

Salt & Sage is a popular restaurant in Velachery, Chennai. It’s known for its warm hospitality, stylish ambience, and a menu that blends North Indian, South Indian, and Chinese dishes. Guests enjoy both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, from soft idlis and pani puri to chicken Chettinad and mutton curry. Desserts like rose rasmalai are also favorites. The restaurant is family-friendly, offers valet parking, and accepts digital payments. Reviews highlight attentive staff and excellent food quality. Average cost for two ranges between ₹1,000–₹2,200, with discounts available through EazyDiner. It’s a great spot for casual dining or special occasions.
