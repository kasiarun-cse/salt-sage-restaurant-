About Salt & Sage

OUR STORY

🍴 Salt and Sage Restaurant: Our Story Since 1999 🍴 1999 – The Beginning: Salt and Sage opened its doors with a vision to serve food that blends essential flavors with wisdom and care. The early years were about creating a welcoming space where guests felt at home. 2000–2005 – Building Foundations: We focused on perfecting recipes, nurturing customer trust, and establishing a reputation for quality. Every dish was crafted with passion, and every guest was treated like family. 2006–2015 – Growth & Innovation: Salt and Sage embraced new culinary trends, expanded its menu, and invested in modern kitchen practices. Our team worked tirelessly to balance tradition with innovation, ensuring every plate told a story. 2016–Present – Transformation: With dedication and teamwork, Salt and Sage evolved into a high‑level restaurant admired for its creativity, consistency, and hospitality. We became more than a place to eat — we became a place to celebrate, connect, and savor life’s flavors. ✨ Our Efforts: From day one, our journey has been fueled by hard work, resilience, and a commitment to excellence. Every challenge was met with determination, every success with gratitude. The efforts of our chefs, staff, and loyal guests have transformed Salt and Sage into a culinary landmark.

OUR TEAM

  • Chef Arjun: With a passion for culinary arts, Chef Arjun brings creativity and precision to every dish. His expertise in blending traditional flavors with modern techniques has earned him accolades and a loyal following.
  • Chef Priya: Known for her innovative approach to cooking, Chef Priya specializes in crafting unique vegetarian dishes that delight the senses. Her dedication to quality ingredients and presentation makes every meal memorable.
  • Chef Ramesh: A master of South Indian cuisine, Chef Ramesh infuses authenticity into every recipe. His deep understanding of spices and cooking methods ensures that each dish is a true representation of regional flavors.
  • Chef Ananya: With a flair for desserts, Chef Ananya creates sweet masterpieces that are both visually stunning and delicious. Her attention to detail and creativity have made her desserts a highlight of our menu.
